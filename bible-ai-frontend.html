What do trusted Christian sources say about Romans 8:28?10:26 AM
Research Summary

Romans 8:28 teaches God’s sovereign assurance that He works all things together for the good of those who love Him and are called according to His purpose. Trusted scholars emphasize that “good” refers to God’s ultimate redeeming plan—conforming to Christ—rather than circumstantial ease, and that the verse anchors believers in God’s faithful, purposeful control even in suffering.

Prototype status

This is a real, responsive frontend component with local interactions only. No Bible provider, model, web research, database, account, or agent runtime is connected yet. Mock source names and Scripture examples are present solely to demonstrate UI states.

Brand and sidebar
Anchors the personal workspace. The sidebar routes between verse lookup, source-grounded research, topical studies, saved notes, history, and build/settings information.
Global search
Searches across chats, verse references, topics, notes, and source titles. It is deliberately separate from the chat composer. Use ⌘/Ctrl + K to focus it.
Version selector
Chooses the preferred Bible translation. “Auto” should let the backend infer a translation from the user’s wording or fall back to the saved default.
Verse Lookup
The default mode for exact references and natural-language passage requests. Production text must come from an authorised Scripture provider, never from model memory.
Research
Starts evidence-led Christian research. The future agent should search approved sources, extract claims, compare views, cite evidence, and label its own synthesis.
Topics
Collects topical studies such as apologetics, prayer, suffering, or justification. Each topic can retain ranked passages, notes, and prior research.
Notes
Stores the user’s own notes and explicitly saved AI outputs. User-authored text should remain distinguishable from generated text.
History
Reopens prior sessions with active passage, selected translation, sources, citations, and tool events intact.
Conversation feed
Shows user prompts and different response types: exact Scripture, topical verse collections, and research summaries.
Scripture card
Provides a canonical reference, translation label, exact verse text, and actions for copy, context, and saving.
Topical result
Ranks relevant passages and briefly shows why they answer the topic. The backend must not invent or paraphrase verse text as if it were a quotation.
Research summary
Shows a concise synthesis. “View Sources” must reveal the evidence used for specific claims, not merely a generic reading list.
Quick Actions
Context-sensitive shortcuts operating on the selected result: copy, compare, open context, and save.
Research Sources
Displays only sources actually used by the current research run, with author/publisher metadata and eventually claim-linked citations.
Composer
Creates the next user message. Suggestion chips add an intent hint without replacing the wording typed by the user.
Theme and profile
The theme switch is functional. The profile is intentionally minimal because this is a personal, single-user app without subscriptions.
Responsive behavior
At tablet width the contextual panel is hidden; on phones the sidebar becomes an off-canvas menu while the main study flow remains usable.
