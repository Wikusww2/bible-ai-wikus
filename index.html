The text behind the text

Old Testament passages open in the Westminster Leningrad Codex (Hebrew, with vowel points); New Testament passages in the Textus Receptus (Koine Greek). The Bible's Aramaic sections — Daniel 2:4–7:28, Ezra 4:8–6:18 & 7:12–26, Jeremiah 10:11 — are preserved inside the same Masoretic manuscript and are detected and labelled automatically, verse by verse. English KJV phrases provide Strong's lookup links where verse numbering agrees with the manuscript — choose a link to open the BDB/Thayer lexicon: lemma, transliteration, pronunciation, and full semantic range. No API key needed for any of this.
